# Pattern: Leadership Team

Use this pattern only when a project has recurring strategic, technical and market responsibilities that benefit from separate mandates. It is not the CPOA default.

## Example ownership

| Topic | CEO / Strategy | CTO / Architecture | CMO / Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| New feature | Is it worth prioritizing now, for whom, and what is deferred? | Can it be built safely; what complexity, technical cost and failure modes exist? | Will it help the right people come, stay or convert; how should it be communicated/measured? |
| Pricing/gating | Revenue, margin, strategic packaging | Enforcement and technical integrity | Customer understanding and conversion/drop-off |
| AI usage | Margin/value at expected usage | Model/architecture quality, reliability and cost | Only when usage materially affects acquisition, retention or messaging |
| Target/positioning | Who should the business serve and what should it be known for? | Technical implications only when material | How do we reach them and help them recognize the value? |

## Rules

- Main Claude remains the normal entry point.
- Default to one relevant director, not the whole team.
- Each director answers only its own question.
- A director may identify questions for another role but must not fill that role's assessment.
- Preserve disagreement. Feasible does not mean strategically valuable; strategically valuable does not mean technically safe.
- Human decisions remain human unless explicitly delegated.

## Additional roles

Do not add CFO, CPO, COO, Legal, Security or other titles because they sound professional. Add one only if it owns a stable recurring question not already owned and separate context, mandate or permissions add real value.
